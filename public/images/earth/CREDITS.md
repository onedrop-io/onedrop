# Earth maps

`day.jpg`, `night.jpg`, and `bump-roughness-clouds.jpg` are by [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

They were packed and resized to 4096×2048 for the [three.js Earth example](https://threejs.org/examples/webgpu_tsl_earth.html) (from the [Three.js Journey](https://threejs-journey.com/lessons/earth-shaders) Earth lesson), then resized again to 2048×1024 and recompressed here.

The home page credits them in its footer. Keep that credit if you use these files.
