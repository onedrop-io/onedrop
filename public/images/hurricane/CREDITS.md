# Hurricane

`isabel.jpg` is made from [NASA Earth Observatory's photo of Hurricane Isabel](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/hurricane-isabel-12116/), taken by MODIS on the Terra satellite on September 8, 2003. Image courtesy Jeff Schmaltz, MODIS Land Rapid Response Team at NASA GSFC.

It's cropped square around the eye, resized to 512×512, turned into a grayscale cloud map (the ocean made black), and faded out toward its edges.

The home page credits it in its footer.
