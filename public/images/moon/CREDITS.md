# Moon maps

`color.jpg` and `elevation.jpg` are from the [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720) by NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, made from data collected by the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter camera and laser altimeter teams. Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio.

`color.jpg` is `lroc_color_poles_2k.tif` resized to 1024×512 and saved as JPEG. `elevation.jpg` is `ldem_3_8bit.jpg`, unchanged.

The home page credits them in its footer. See [NASA's media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).
