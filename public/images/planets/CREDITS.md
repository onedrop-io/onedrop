# Planet maps

`mercury.jpg`, `venus.jpg` (its clouds), `jupiter.jpg`, `saturn.jpg`, `saturn-ring.png`, `uranus.jpg`, and `neptune.jpg` are Solar System Scope's [2K maps](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), resized to 1024 px wide and recompressed.

The home page credits them in its footer. Keep that credit if you use these files. The Sun in the Solar System view isn't a map: it's drawn by a shader in `resources/js/components/home/solar-closeup.tsx`.
