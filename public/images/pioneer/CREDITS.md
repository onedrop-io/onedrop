# Pioneer

- `pioneer.glb` is NASA's [Pioneer 10 model](https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources/tree/master/3D%20Models/Pioneer%2010) from NASA's 3D Resources ("free and without copyright"), decompressed and with its textures shrunk to 1024 px WebP by glTF-Transform.
- `plaque.svg` is [Oona Räisänen's tracing](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pioneer_plaque.svg) of NASA's photo of the Pioneer plaque (public domain), designed by Carl Sagan and Frank Drake, drawn by Linda Salzman Sagan.

The home page credits them in its footer.
