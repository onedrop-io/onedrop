# Mars map

`color.jpg` is Solar System Scope's [2K Mars map](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), recompressed as a smaller JPEG.

The home page credits it in its footer. Keep that credit if you use this file.

The Mars surface in the close-up (Gale Crater, Mount Sharp, the rocks, and the rover's shadow) isn't made from any image: it's drawn by a shader in `resources/js/components/home/mars-closeup.tsx`.
