# Voyager

- `voyager.glb` is NASA's [Voyager 3D model](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/voyager-3d-model/) from NASA's 3D Resources ("free and without copyright"), with its textures shrunk to 1024 px WebP by glTF-Transform.
- `golden-record.jpg` is NASA/JPL's photo of the Golden Record cover, [GPN-2000-001978](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Voyager_Golden_Record.jpg) (public domain), resized to 1600 px.

The home page credits them in its footer.
