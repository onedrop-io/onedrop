OneDrop turns a description like "a time-off tracker for my team" into a working web app. An AI coding agent builds it as real, tested code inside an isolated sandbox, and the app runs live in a preview next to the chat while the agent works.

What sets it apart:

- **Built for production.** The agent writes standard code (React, or Laravel with a database when the app needs one), runs the tests, and has sign-in, secrets, analytics, and feature flags built in. It's code a developer can review and extend.
- **Bring your own subscription.** Start on free AI credits at onedrop.io, then build on your Claude Pro or Max plan or your ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan, or your own key for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter, or Ollama. You pay your provider directly, with no markup.
- **Deploy anywhere.** Publish to OneDrop's hosting, your own Fly.io and Cloudflare accounts, your own domain, or your team's Tailscale network, or self-host OneDrop on your laptop, any Ubuntu server, or AWS. Nothing is locked to one host or one framework.
- **Free to self-host.** OneDrop is source available under the [Elastic License 2.0](https://www.elastic.co/licensing/elastic-license): use it, change it, and run it for your own team or company. The one thing you can't do is offer it to others as a hosted service.

## Ways to use it

- **In your browser.** Sign up at [onedrop.io](https://onedrop.io) and start building on free AI credits, with nothing to install. This is where most people start.
- **On your desktop.** The [desktop app](/guides/desktop-app) for macOS, Windows and Linux signs in to your onedrop.io account, or to a OneDrop your team runs.
- **On your own servers.** Self-host it for free. On macOS or Linux, with Docker running:

    ```bash
    curl -fsSL https://onedrop.io/install | sh
    ```

    See [Install on your computer](/install) for what it does, or [install on a server](/self-hosting/install-anywhere) for your team. A self-hosted OneDrop has no marketing home page: its address opens the log-in page, or your projects once you're signed in.

## How it works

<Steps>
    <Step title="Connect your AI">
        Use your Claude or ChatGPT plan, add an API key (Ollama included), or
        sign in with OpenRouter. See [Set up your AI](/guides/set-up-ai).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Describe your app">
        Type what you want to build. OneDrop creates a project and a sandbox for
        it. See [Build a project](/guides/build-a-project).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Watch it get built">
        The agent reads, writes, and runs code in the sandbox. You see each step
        in the chat and the app in the preview.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Share it">
        Publish the project to give it its own URL, private to your team or
        public. See [Publish a project](/guides/publish).
    </Step>
</Steps>

## Who it's for

OneDrop is built for trusted teams: people inside a company who want to build internal tools without setting up a development environment. It is not designed to host apps for anonymous strangers.
