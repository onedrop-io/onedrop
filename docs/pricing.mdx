OneDrop charges for teams and hosting, never for your AI. Connect your own AI on any plan and it costs exactly what your provider charges. If you don't have your own, paid plans include monthly AI credits.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Builders | Always-on apps | AI credits each month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Self-hosted | Free | Unlimited | Unlimited | Bring your own |
| Solo | $20/month, or $16/month billed yearly | 1 | 3 | $10 |
| Team | $99/month, or $79/month billed yearly | Up to 10 | 20 | $50, shared |
| Business | $299/month, or $239/month billed yearly | Up to 50 | 50 | $200, shared |

- **Builders** are people who create or change apps. Anyone can open a published app, for free.
- **Always-on apps** are published apps kept running with their own link. There's no limit on projects.
- Business adds sign-in with Google, GitHub, or Microsoft, and priority support.

## Bring your own AI

On every plan, each person can [connect their own AI](/guides/set-up-ai): a ChatGPT Plus or Pro plan, or an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or OpenRouter. They pay their provider directly, and OneDrop adds nothing on top. People who connect their own AI never use the plan's credits.

## Included AI credits

- Credits cover model usage at provider rates, for anyone on the plan who hasn't connected their own AI.
- Unused credits roll over for one month.
- You can top up any time, at provider rates.
- Hosting never uses credits. If credits run out, building waits for a top-up, and published apps keep running.

## Self-hosting

OneDrop is free to self-host under the [Elastic License 2.0](https://www.elastic.co/licensing/elastic-license). [Install it](/self-hosting/install-anywhere) on your laptop, any Ubuntu server, or [AWS](/self-hosting/deploy-aws) with every feature and no limits. You pay only for your servers and your AI.
