## Account menu

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open the account menu:

| Item              | What it does                                                                                             |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Settings**      | Opens the settings window.                                                                               |
| **Usage**         | Tokens and estimated cost of your agent runs. See [Usage](/guides/usage).                                |
| **Invite people** | Create an invite link. See [Invite people](/guides/invite-people).                                       |
| **Theme**         | Switch between **Light**, **Dark** (the default), and **System**. The current theme is shown next to it. |
| **Help**          | Links to this documentation and the OneDrop repository.                                                  |
| **Log out**       | Signs you out.                                                                                           |

## Settings

Settings open in a window over the page you're on. Pick a section on the left:

- **Account**: **Profile** (name, email, delete your account), **Security** (password, two-factor, passkeys, connected logins), **AI** (see [Set up AI](/guides/set-up-ai)), **Appearance**, and **Notifications**.
- **People**: **Groups** and **Invite people**. See [Groups and users](/guides/groups-and-users).
- **Admin** (admins only): **Users**.

Close the window with **×** or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to go back to where you were. Each section has its own address (for example `/settings/profile` or `/groups`), so you can link straight to it.

## Desktop notifications

Turn on desktop notifications to hear when a project is ready for you to review, even while you're on another page or another tab.

While the agent is working, the chat offers to turn them on: click **Turn on**, then **Allow** when your browser asks. Click **Not now** to stop being asked in this browser.

You can also turn them on (or off) any time:

1. Open **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Switch on **Desktop notifications**, then click **Allow** when your browser asks.

Right after you allow them, you get a **Notifications are on** notification. If it doesn't appear, your computer is blocking them. On a Mac, check **System Settings → Notifications** for your browser, and turn off Focus or Do Not Disturb.

When a project's agent finishes working, you get a notification with the project's name. Click it to open the project. You aren't notified about the project you're already looking at.

<Note>
    The setting is saved in your browser, so turn it on in each browser you use.
    Keep the app open in a tab: notifications come from that tab.
</Note>

If the switch is greyed out and says notifications are blocked, allow them in your browser's site settings (the icon next to the address), then come back to **Settings → Notifications**.
