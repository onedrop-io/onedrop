## Account menu

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to open the account menu:

| Item              | What it does                                                                                             |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Settings**      | Opens the settings window.                                                                               |
| **Usage**         | Tokens and estimated cost of your agent runs. See [Usage](/guides/usage).                                |
| **Invite people** | Create an invite link. See [Invite people](/guides/invite-people).                                       |
| **Theme**         | Switch between **Light**, **Dark** (the default), and **System**, or open **Color theme…** to pick a [color theme](#color-themes). |
| **Mode**          | Switch between **Simple** and **Advanced**. See [Simple and Advanced modes](/guides/build-a-project#simple-and-advanced-modes). |
| **Help**          | Links to this documentation and the OneDrop repository.                                                  |
| **Log out**       | Signs you out.                                                                                           |

## Settings

Settings open in a window over the page you're on. Pick a section on the left:

- **Account**: **Profile** (name, email, delete your account), **Security** (password, two-factor, passkeys, connected logins), **AI** (see [Set up AI](/guides/set-up-ai)), **Appearance**, and **Notifications**.
- **Organization**: **General**, **Members**, **Groups**, **Invite people**, **Secrets** and **Hosting accounts**. Members see only **Members**, **Groups** and **Invite people**. See [Organizations](/guides/organizations) and [Groups and users](/guides/groups-and-users).
- **Admin** (admins only): **Users**, **General**, **Sandboxes**, **Monitoring**, **Server**, and **Backups**, plus **Reviews** on the hosted install. See [Administer OneDrop](/self-hosting/administration).

Close the window with **×** or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to go back to where you were. Each section has its own address (for example `/settings/profile` or `/groups`), so you can link straight to it.

## Color themes

OneDrop comes in its own dark and light themes, plus versions of popular editor themes. Open **Settings → Appearance**, or **Theme → Color theme…** in the account menu, and click a theme. It applies straight away.

| Dark | Light |
| --- | --- |
| OneDrop Dark (the default), One Dark Pro, Dracula, GitHub Dark, Monokai, Nord, Tokyo Night, Catppuccin Mocha, Solarized Dark, Gruvbox Dark, Night Owl, Rosé Pine | OneDrop Light, GitHub Light, One Light, Solarized Light, Catppuccin Latte, Rosé Pine Dawn |

You keep one dark theme and one light theme:

- Clicking a dark theme switches to dark mode, and a light theme to light mode.
- On **System**, OneDrop shows your dark theme when your computer is in dark mode and your light theme when it's in light mode.

The theme covers the whole app: pages and menus, code in the chat and in diffs, the file editor and SQL runner, logs and consoles, and the [Shell](/guides/workspace).

<Note>
    Your theme is saved in your browser, so pick it in each browser you use.
</Note>

## Desktop notifications

Turn on desktop notifications to hear when a project is ready for you to review, even while you're on another page or another tab.

While the agent is working, the chat offers to turn them on: click **Turn on**, then **Allow** when your browser asks. Click **Not now** to stop being asked in this browser.

You can also turn them on (or off) any time:

1. Open **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Switch on **Desktop notifications**, then click **Allow** when your browser asks.

Right after you allow them, you get a **Notifications are on** notification. If it doesn't appear, your computer is blocking them. On a Mac, check **System Settings → Notifications** for your browser, and turn off Focus or Do Not Disturb.

When a project's agent finishes working, you get a notification with the project's name and **Ready for your review**. When the agent is [waiting for you](/guides/build-a-project#your-projects) instead, it says so: **Needs your answer**, **Needs something from you**, or **Got stuck and needs your help** (this takes a moment longer, while the turn is checked). Click it to open the project. You aren't notified about the project you're already looking at.

<Note>
    The setting is saved in your browser, so turn it on in each browser you use.
    Keep the app open in a tab: notifications come from that tab.
</Note>

If the switch is greyed out and says notifications are blocked, allow them in your browser's site settings (the icon next to the address), then come back to **Settings → Notifications**.
