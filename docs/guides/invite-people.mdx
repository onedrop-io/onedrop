---
title: 'Invite people'
description: 'Create a single-use invite link and send it to a teammate.'
---

Anyone signed in can invite others to their [organization](/guides/organizations) from **Invite people** in the account menu (click your name at the bottom of the sidebar), or under **People** in [Settings](/guides/settings).

## Create an invite

<Steps>
    <Step title="Enter their email (optional)">
        Leave it blank for a link anyone can use once.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Click Create invite link">
        The invite appears in the list below.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Click Copy link">
        Send the link to them however you like. OneDrop doesn't send emails.
    </Step>
</Steps>

Each link works once and expires after 7 days.

## What they see

The link opens the sign-up page with a note saying who invited them, and their email filled in if the invite had one. Signing up puts them in your organization. If they sign up with the invited email, they skip email verification.

Used, expired, and revoked links explain why they no longer work.

## Manage invites

The list shows each invite's status: **Waiting**, **Accepted** (and who joined), **Expired**, or **Revoked**. Revoke a waiting invite to cancel it. Organization admins see every invite in the organization.

<Note>
    Sign-up is open to everyone. Invites are a convenient way to bring people
    in, not a requirement.
</Note>
