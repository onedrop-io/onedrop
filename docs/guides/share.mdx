---
title: 'Share a project'
description: 'Show off what you built: a public page with your prompt, a screenshot, and a preview card for social posts.'
---

Sharing gives a project a public page on OneDrop, such as `https://onedrop.example.com/s/team-crm-k3x9q2ab`. The page shows the prompt you started from, a screenshot of the app, and a **Remix this** button so anyone can start their own project from your prompt.

Links to the page unfurl in Slack, X, LinkedIn, iMessage and elsewhere with a preview card that OneDrop makes for the project: your prompt next to a screenshot of the app.

<Info>
    Sharing is separate from [publishing](/guides/publish). The share page shows
    a screenshot, not the running app. When the project is also published as
    **Public**, the page links to the app with **Open the app**.
</Info>

## Share

<Steps>
    <Step title="Open the Share panel">
        Click **Share** at the top right of the workspace.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Choose what to show">
        **Prompt to show** starts as the project's first prompt. Edit it so it
        reads well and leaves out anything private. **Page to screenshot** is
        the path in the app to take the screenshot of, such as `/` or
        `/dashboard`.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Share">
        Click **Share**. OneDrop takes the screenshot and makes the card in the
        background, which takes a few seconds. The panel shows the card when
        it's ready.
    </Step>
</Steps>

The panel then shows the link with a copy button, **Post on X** and **LinkedIn** buttons, and how many views and remixes the page has had. Views from you and from link-preview bots aren't counted.

## What the page shows

- The project's name and your first name
- The prompt you chose
- A screenshot of the app
- How many prompts the project took, and which agent built it
- **Remix this**, plus **Open the app** when the project is published as Public

The rest of the chat, the code, and the app's data are never shown.

## Update the card

The card and screenshot don't change by themselves when the app does. After changing the app, open **Share** and click **Refresh card**. Saving a new prompt or page also makes a new card.

<Note>
    Making a card needs the project's sandbox and app to be running. If they
    aren't, the panel says why. Open the project, wait for the preview, and
    click **Refresh card**. Until the first card is ready, links show OneDrop's
    own card.
</Note>

## Waiting for a review

On OneDrop's hosted service, shared projects are checked for phishing, scams and malware, like [public apps](/guides/publish#waiting-for-a-review). If the check flags a project, its share page is hidden and the **Share** panel says it's waiting for a review. It comes back once someone at OneDrop approves it. A project that's taken down loses its share page and can't be shared again.

## Remix

Anyone on a share page can click **Remix this**. They land on the new-project page with the shared prompt filled in, ready to edit and send. Visitors without an account sign up first, and set up AI if they need to.

## Stop sharing

Open **Share** and click **Stop sharing**. The page, its card and its screenshot stop working right away. Sharing again gives the project a new link. Deleting a project also deletes its share page.

## Self-hosting

OneDrop makes cards with headless Chromium inside the sandbox image. After updating, rebuild the image with `php artisan sandbox:build-image`. Sandboxes pick up the new image the next time their project is opened.
