Drive holds your files and the files you share. Open it with **Drive** in the sidebar.

## Places

| Place                         | Who sees it                                                                  |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **My Drive**                  | Only you.                                                                    |
| Your organization's drive     | Everyone in the organization.                                                |
| Each of your groups' drives   | The group's members. Organization owners and admins see every group's drive. |

To share a file, put it in your organization's drive or a group's.

## Files and folders

- **Upload** files or a whole folder, or drag them onto the page.
- **New folder**, then drag files onto a folder to move them, or use **Move to…** from a file's menu to move it to another folder or place.
- Click a file to open it. Images, video, audio and PDFs show in the page; text and code open in an editor you can save from (⌘S / Ctrl+S). Anything else offers a download.
- **Download** a file, or a folder as a zip.

Uploading a file with the same name as one in the folder replaces it with the new version.

Changes made anywhere else, by someone else, on a computer or by a project's agent, show up without reloading.

## Trash

Deleting a file or folder, from the page, a computer or a project, moves it to that place's **Trash** for 30 days. From there you can **Restore** it (beside anything that took its name meanwhile) or delete it for good. After 30 days it's deleted for good.

## Drive on your computer

Your [computer](/guides/computer) keeps your drives in its **Drive** folder (`~/Drive`, also on its desktop):

| Folder                 | Place                     |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------- |
| `My Drive`             | My Drive                  |
| `<organization name>`  | Your organization's drive |
| `Groups/<group name>`  | Each group's drive        |

Files you or the AI add, change, rename or delete there reach Drive within seconds, and Drive's changes reach your computer within seconds while you're using it. When a file is changed in two places at once, both are kept: the other copy is saved beside it as `<name> (conflict <date>)`.

## Drive in projects

A project's agent and Shell find your organization's drive and the drives of the project owner's groups at `/drive` (`/drive/<organization name>`, `/drive/Groups/<group name>`). They can read and write them, so an agent can use shared specs, assets and data, and save what it makes. The owner's My Drive isn't there.

`/drive` is outside the project's files, so it's never committed, backed up with the code or published.

## Space

The Drive page shows how much space your organization's files take, Trash included. The hosted OneDrop has a limit per organization; an upload that would go over it says so. Empty Trash to make room.
