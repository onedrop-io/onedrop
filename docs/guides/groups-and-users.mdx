## Groups

Open **Groups** in [Settings](/guides/settings) to see the groups you belong to in your current [organization](/guides/organizations). Each group belongs to one organization.

- Anyone can create a group and becomes its owner
- Owners can add people from the same organization by email, change roles between owner and member, remove members, rename the group, and delete it
- Members can leave a group
- A group always keeps at least one owner

## Admins

Admins see **Users** under **Admin** in [Settings](/guides/settings). From there they can:

- See every user and how many groups they're in
- Click a user to see everything about them: their account (last sign-in and how, who invited them, verification, password, two-factor, passkeys), organizations, groups, projects with their sandboxes and any sandbox, publish or git errors, AI connections, sign-in providers, GitHub, invites they sent, agent skills, SSH keys, and recent sessions. Secrets such as AI keys are never shown.
- See a user's AI usage: all-time cost and tokens, a daily cost chart for the past 30 days, and which models and projects it went to
- **Sign out everywhere** to end every session a user has, or **Reset two-factor** for someone who lost their device. Neither works on your own account; use Security settings for that.
- **Impersonate** a user to see the app as they do. A banner at the bottom of every page shows who you're signed in as, with **Stop impersonating** to go back to your own account. You can't impersonate yourself or another admin, and while impersonating you can't change the user's profile, password, two-factor, passkeys or sign-in providers, or delete their account. Each impersonation (who, from which IP, when it started and stopped) is listed on the user's page.
- Grant or revoke admin access for other users (not themselves)

On a self-hosted install, admins also run its one [organization](/guides/organizations): they can see and manage every group, project, and invite in it. They change install-wide settings too. See [Administer OneDrop](/self-hosting/administration).

The seeded dev user (`dev@example.com`) is an admin.
