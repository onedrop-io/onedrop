---
title: 'Publish a project'
description: 'Give a project its own URL, private to your team or public to anyone.'
---

Publishing gives a project its own address. On a [server install](/install#install-on-a-server) it can be on your own domain, such as `https://timesheets-4.onedrop.example.com`. Anywhere, including a laptop, it can be on your team's [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com) network, such as `https://timesheets-4.your-tailnet.ts.net`.

<Info>
    Publishing shares the live sandbox, including changes the agent is still
    making. It is not a separate production build.
</Info>

## Publish

<Steps>
    <Step title="Open the Publish panel">
        Click **Publish** at the top right of the workspace.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Choose where (servers only)">
        On a server, pick **Your domain** or **Tailscale**. Elsewhere it's
        always Tailscale.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Choose who can open it">
        **Private** or **Public** (see the table below).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Click Publish">
        The status changes to **Publishing…** and then shows the URL with a copy
        button.
    </Step>
</Steps>

|             | Your domain                         | Tailscale                                              |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Private** | Anyone in the project's organization | People on your team's tailnet (Tailscale Serve)        |
| **Public**  | Anyone on the internet with the URL | Anyone on the internet with the URL (Tailscale Funnel) |

Someone who opens a private app on your domain without being signed in is sent to sign in to OneDrop first, then back to the page they asked for. They need to be in the project's [organization](/guides/organizations), but don't need to set up an AI or be part of the project.

If OneDrop has no Tailscale auth key configured, publishing to Tailscale shows **Approve in Tailscale**. Open the link, approve the device in your Tailscale account, and publishing continues on its own. You only approve each project once.

## Republish or unpublish

Once published, the button says **Republish**. Use it to switch between Private and Public, or between your domain and Tailscale (the project comes off the one it was on). **Unpublish** takes the project offline.

The **Tools → Publishing** section shows the same status, URL, and who published it.

## Works with any framework

Published URLs reach your app as `localhost`, so frameworks that block unknown hostnames (such as Vite and Next.js dev servers) work without extra configuration.

Setting up Tailscale for your server is covered in [Tailscale publishing](/self-hosting/tailscale).
