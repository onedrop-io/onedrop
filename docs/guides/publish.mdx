---
title: 'Publish a project'
description: 'Give a project its own URL, private to your team or public to anyone.'
---

Publishing gives a project its own address, such as `https://timesheets-4.your-tailnet.ts.net`, using [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com). It works even when OneDrop runs on a laptop.

<Info>
    Publishing shares the live sandbox, including changes the agent is still
    making. It is not a separate production build.
</Info>

## Publish

<Steps>
    <Step title="Open the Publish panel">
        Click **Publish** at the top right of the workspace.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Choose who can open it">
        - **Private**: people on your team's tailnet (Tailscale Serve) -
        **Public**: anyone on the internet with the URL (Tailscale Funnel)
    </Step>
    <Step title="Click Publish">
        The status changes to **Publishing…** and then shows the URL with a copy
        button.
    </Step>
</Steps>

If OneDrop has no Tailscale auth key configured, the panel shows **Approve in Tailscale**. Open the link, approve the device in your Tailscale account, and publishing continues on its own. You only approve each project once.

## Republish or unpublish

Once published, the button says **Republish**. Use it to switch between Private and Public. **Unpublish** takes the project offline.

The **Tools → Publishing** section shows the same status, URL, and who published it.

## Works with any framework

Published URLs reach your app as `localhost`, so frameworks that block unknown hostnames (such as Vite and Next.js dev servers) work without extra configuration.

Setting up Tailscale for your server is covered in [Tailscale publishing](/self-hosting/tailscale).
