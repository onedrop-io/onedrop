title: 'Apps'
description: 'Find every app your organization has published in one place, pin the ones you use, and sort them by group.'
---

**Apps** in the sidebar lists every app your organization's people have published, so you don't need to remember an app's address to find it. You can open it without setting up an AI first.

## What's listed

An app shows up by itself once it's published somewhere everyone in the organization can open it:

| Published to                | Listed                                     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Your domain, Private        | Yes. Anyone in the organization opens it.  |
| Your domain or Hosting, Public | Yes. Anyone with the link opens it.     |
| Tailscale, Public           | Yes.                                       |
| Tailscale, Private          | No. Only people on the tailnet could open it. |

An app that's waiting for a review, failed to publish or was unpublished isn't listed.

Each tile shows the app's icon, name, address, owner, groups, and whether it's **Private** (your organization) or **Public**. Click it to open the app in a new tab. Its owner and the organization's owners and admins also find **Open project** in its **⋯** menu. Everyone else sees the app, not its project: its chat, code and tools stay private.

Search by an app's name or its owner's name.

## Pin your apps

Click the pin on a tile (or **Pin to Your apps** in its **⋯** menu) to keep it in **Your apps** at the top of the page. Pins are yours: nobody else sees them.

## Groups

An app's owner can put it in their [groups](/guides/groups-and-users) from its **⋯** menu → **Groups…**. Organization owners and admins can put any app in any group. Once apps are in groups, click a group above the tiles to see only its apps.

## Hide or feature an app

- **Hide from Apps**: the app's owner and the organization's owners and admins can take it off the page from its **⋯** menu, or with the **Apps** switch in the [Publish](/guides/publish) panel. A hidden app still works at its address; only they see it, under **Hidden from Apps**, where **List in Apps** brings it back.
- **Feature**: organization owners and admins can feature an app so it comes first for everyone, marked with a star.
